## [SCENE 1] Hook (0–5s)
<!-- Visual: close-up of an octopus pulsing on the sea floor -->
An octopus has three hearts. And when it swims, one of them stops.

## [SCENE 2] Setup
<!-- Visual: simple animated diagram of an octopus body -->
So why would any animal need three?

## [SCENE 3] Main point 1
<!-- Visual: diagram highlighting the two gill hearts -->
Two small hearts sit next to the gills. Their only job is to push blood through the gills to pick up oxygen.

## [SCENE 4] Main point 2
<!-- Visual: diagram highlighting the central heart, arrows to the arms -->
The third, bigger heart sends that oxygen-rich blood to the rest of the body, all the way out to eight arms.

## [SCENE 5] Main point 3 / twist
<!-- Visual: octopus swimming, then crawling over rocks; blue blood graphic -->
Their blood is blue, because it carries copper instead of iron. And when an octopus swims, that main heart pauses. That's one reason octopuses would rather crawl.

## [SCENE 6] Payoff & call to action
<!-- Visual: octopus disappearing into a crevice; channel logo -->
Three hearts, blue blood, and a body that prefers walking. Follow for more strange facts about the ocean.
