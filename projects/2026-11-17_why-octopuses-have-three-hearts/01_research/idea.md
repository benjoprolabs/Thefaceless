## One-sentence pitch
Octopuses have three hearts and blue blood, and one of those hearts stops when they swim.

## Target viewer
Curious viewers aged 13–35 who like quick "nature is weird" facts.

## Hook angle
"An octopus has three hearts, and when it swims, one of them stops beating."

## Key points (3–5)
1. Two small hearts (branchial hearts) pump blood through the gills.
2. One main heart (systemic heart) pumps blood to the rest of the body.
3. Their blood is blue because it uses copper-based hemocyanin, not iron-based hemoglobin.
4. The main heart pauses while swimming, so octopuses often prefer crawling.
