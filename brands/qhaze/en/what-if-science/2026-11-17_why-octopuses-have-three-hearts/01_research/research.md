## One-sentence pitch
Octopuses have three hearts and blue blood, and their main heart pauses when they swim.

## Target viewer
Curious viewers aged 13–35 who enjoy quick "nature is weird" facts.

## Key findings
1. Two branchial hearts pump blood through the gills.
2. One systemic heart pumps oxygenated blood to the body.
3. Blood is blue because it carries copper-based hemocyanin.
4. The systemic heart pauses while swimming; crawling is less tiring.
