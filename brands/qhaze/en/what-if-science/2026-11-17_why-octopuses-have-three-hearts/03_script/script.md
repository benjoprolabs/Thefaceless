## [SCENE 1] Hook
<!-- Visual: close-up of an octopus on the sea floor -->
An octopus has three hearts [F1]. And when it swims, one of them stops [F2].


## [SCENE 2] Setup
<!-- Visual: simple animated octopus diagram -->
So why would any animal need three? Two small hearts sit beside the gills and push blood through them to collect oxygen [F3].


## [SCENE 3] Main point
<!-- Visual: diagram of central heart, arrows to the arms; blue blood graphic -->
The third, bigger heart pumps that blood to the rest of the body, all the way to eight arms. And the blood is blue, because it carries copper instead of iron [F4].


## [SCENE 4] Payoff & call to action
<!-- Visual: octopus crawling over rocks, then channel logo -->
That main heart pauses while the octopus swims, so swimming tires it out. That's why octopuses would rather crawl. Follow for more strange ocean facts.
